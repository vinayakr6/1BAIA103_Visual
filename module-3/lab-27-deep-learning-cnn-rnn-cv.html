INTERACT: CNN vs RNN vs Feedforward

Feedforward

Information moves in one direction from input to output.

CNN

Specialised for image recognition and processing.

RNN

Used for sequence-based data such as time series or speech.

Computer Vision

Uses image-processing and learning methods to interpret visual information.

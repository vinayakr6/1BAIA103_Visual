INTERACT: Advantages vs Disadvantages

AdvantagesDisadvantages / concerns
Can process large amounts of data quicklyCan require substantial data, computation or engineering
Can automate repetitive tasksOutputs can be wrong or biased
Can support decision makingRaises issues of accountability, employment and responsible use
Can operate consistently on defined tasksTask-specific systems do not automatically possess general human intelligence

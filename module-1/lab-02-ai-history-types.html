SEE: AI as an evolving field

1940s–1950s

Early mathematical and computational ideas laid foundations for artificial intelligence.

1956

The Dartmouth workshop is commonly associated with the formal naming of the field of artificial intelligence.

1960s–1970s

Symbolic AI, problem solving and early language/knowledge systems developed.

1980s

Expert systems became an important practical AI approach.

1990s–2000s

Data-driven machine learning and statistical methods became increasingly important.

2010s–today

Deep learning, large-scale data and generative AI have expanded AI capabilities and applications.

INTERACT: Five functionality-based types

Reactive Machines

Respond to current input; no stored experience used to form a memory of past events.

Limited Memory

Uses past information for a limited purpose, such as recent observations or learned patterns.

Theory of Mind

A proposed capability involving understanding beliefs, intentions and emotions of others.

Self-Awareness

A hypothetical level involving awareness of oneself.

Capability view

Weak and strong AI classify systems by breadth of intelligence rather than memory mechanism.

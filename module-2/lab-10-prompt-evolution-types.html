Early practice

Users informally experimented with input formats to elicit desired outputs.

Systematic techniques

Instruction-based, role, seed-word, conditional, zero-shot, one-shot, few-shot and self-consistency approaches emerged.

LLM era

Advanced language models expanded opportunities for prompt engineering across communication and application domains.

Future direction

The sources point to automated prompt generation, adaptive/dynamic prompts and greater contextual awareness.

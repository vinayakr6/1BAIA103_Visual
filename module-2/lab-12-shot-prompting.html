When is each useful?

TechniqueSource-based ideaLimitation
Zero-shotOpen-ended tasks or when you want the model to use general knowledge.May be less accurate or relevant without context.
One-shotUseful when one example can clarify the task.Effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the example.
Few-shotSeveral examples give more context and guidance.Examples must be representative; too many or poor examples may not help.

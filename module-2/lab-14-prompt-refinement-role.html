Prompt Refinement & Role Prompting

Iterate • Clarify • Add constraints • Change audience/role

SEE: Refinement is iterative

1 · AMBIGUOUS

Tell me about the water cycle.

Broad; output may be generic or unfocused.

2 · CLEARER

Explain the water cycle briefly with its main stages.

Adds scope and desired content.

3 · SPECIFIC

Explain the water cycle in 4–5 short bullet points for 8th grade students, using simple words.

Adds audience, format, length and language constraints.

INTERACT: Role Prompting

The supplied activity asks students to explain Newton’s Laws from three roles and compare tone, detail and style.

Expert Engineer

Technical depth and engineering applications.

High School Teacher

Simple, structured, everyday examples.

Beginner

Very basic, casual explanation.

Refinement checklist

  • What exactly should the model do?
  • Who is the audience?
  • What context is missing?
  • What format, length or constraints are required?
  • What was confusing in the previous response?
Exam Corner

For a long-answer prompt-engineering question, explain the purpose → technique → prompt structure → example → expected effect → limitation. Include the prompt itself where appropriate.

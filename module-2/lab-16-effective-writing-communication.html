Five writing prompt types

TypePurpose
CreativeEncourages imagination and creativity for fiction, poetry or storytelling.
DescriptiveDevelops detailed description of people, places or objects.
Problem-solvingSupports challenges and critical thinking in writing.
ReflectiveEncourages exploration of thoughts, experiences and feelings.
PersuasiveHelps practise convincing readers of a viewpoint.

Communication connection

The reference book explains that prompts can improve specificity and clarity, adapt communication to different contexts, increase creativity and engagement, and help overcome linguistic barriers. It also stresses ethical considerations such as bias and manipulation.

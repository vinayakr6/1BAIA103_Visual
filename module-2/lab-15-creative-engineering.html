Useful creative prompt moves

Change perspective

Ask how different stakeholders might see the same problem.

Combine ideas

Ask for combinations of two seemingly unrelated concepts.

Add constraints

Use limited resources, sustainability, cost or accessibility to make ideas practical.

Explore failure

Ask what could fail and how that failure could become a learning input.
